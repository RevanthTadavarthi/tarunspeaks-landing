# Product Requirement Document (PRD) — RMDM Premium Upgrade

## 1. Product Positioning
- **Target Audience:** Highly advanced marketing operators, growth leads, and founders.
- **Price Point:** Premium cohort pricing (₹97,000 / $1,200).
- **Aesthetic Core:** Minimalist, clean-border aligned, monochrome-heavy, typography-first user experience. Elite editorial style.

## 2. Functional & Content Scoping
- **Hero Area:** Consolidate active stats (13 batches, 28,300+ operators, 30 seats) directly into the core copy block layout to drop separate, cluttered trust bands.
- **Problem Formulation:** Emphasize "The Fragment Trap" frameworks rather than open-ended leading bullet items or floating element modules.
- **Curriculum Architecture:** Enforce a high-contrast 2x2 structural matrix detailing the 4 premium modules (M1 to M4) and explicitly map out the actionable deliverables.
- **Conversion Vector:** Maintain high-barrier admission standards. Visual language must communicate "Application Only" selection rather than generic retail transactions.
